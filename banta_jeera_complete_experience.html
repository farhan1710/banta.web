The Heritage

The Soul of Delhi Streets, Reimagined

Patented in 1872 by Hiram Codd, the marble-sealed soda bottle took India by storm. Street-side pushcart vendors (banta-wallahs) perfected their personal blend of hand-pounded spices to beat the blistering North Indian heat. We carry on this timeless alchemy with farm-direct ingredients and modern artisanal hygiene.

1872 • The Codd-Neck Invention

Hiram Codd introduces the glass marble seal held tight by natural carbon gas pressure.

1920s • The Street-Side Revolution

Old Delhi stalls infuse cumin, mint leaves, and black rock salt, giving rise to Banta Jeera.

1980s • Golden Street Era

Every college canteen and cinema hall echoes with the unmistakable pop of marble bottles.

Today • The Modern Fusion

Micro-filtered Himalayan spring water, vacuum-toasted cumin, and zero compromise.
